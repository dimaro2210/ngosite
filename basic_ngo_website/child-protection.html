Official Statement by Arnold Nicholas

"Arnold Nicholas is a child advocate of Saferplace Initiative, standing to advocate for the rights of children across the globe.

Arnold believes in creating a safe space where children can thrive to become what they want to be.

Arnold stands to advocate for child rights, for child protection, for child access to basic life amenities such as:

  • Access to Education
  • Access to Safe and Potable Drinking Water
  • Access to Nutritional-Based Food
  • Access to a Good Hospital & Healthcare

Join me, Arnold Nicholas, as I advocate for these things for children across the globe. Thank you."

1. Access to Education

Every child deserves quality schooling, standard learning kits, and an uninterrupted academic path to reach their dreams.

2. Access to Safe & Potable Water

Safe drinking water is essential to life. We construct clean school wash blocks and community water points to prevent water-borne disease.

3. Access to Nutritional-Based Food

Shielding children from malnutrition and starvation by delivering therapeutic supplementary foods and growth screening.

4. Access to a Good Hospital

Free pediatric clinical checkups, routine childhood immunizations, and emergency medication for rural children.

What Arnold Nicholas Stands Firmly Against

Child Labor Ending street hawking, quarry labor, and commercial exploitation of minors.
Child Trafficking Combating illegal trafficking rings and safeguarding vulnerable and displaced children.
Starvation & Severe Hunger Immediate clinical nutrition intervention and food support for impoverished families.
Deprivation & Abuse Protecting minors from physical violence, sexual abuse, and lack of birth registration.
